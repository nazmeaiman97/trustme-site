The game tells you what to do.
It's lying.

A troll platformer. 60 levels, the first 40 free, no ads.

How it works

  • Before every level, the Host (a smug purple showman with a monocle) tells you exactly what to do.
  • He's lying. Floors vanish, doors run away, and even the LEVEL COMPLETE screen is fake.
  • Record your own death scream: the game plays it every time you die.
  • Duo mode: the game blames your friend.

In the store, search "trust me fair game".
