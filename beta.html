Play it on Android before launch

Google Play needs testers before the game can launch. It's free, it takes 2 minutes, and the first 40 levels have no ads.

  1. Join the tester group, signed in with the Google account your phone's Play Store uses.
    Join the group
  2. Opt in to the test and tap "Become a tester".
    Opt in
  3. Install from Google Play (it can take a few minutes to show up).
    Install

Keep it installed for 14 days and open it now and then: that's what lets it launch for everyone. Stuck? Email nazmeaimandev@gmail.com.
