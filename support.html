Common questions

  • I bought THE FINALE but levels 51 to 60 are locked. Open Settings (the gear) and tap RESTORE PURCHASE, or tap RESTORE on the finale card. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.
  • How do I record my own voice? On the title screen, tap VOICE, pick a sound, and hold HOLD while you talk. Let go to save it; slide off to cancel. If the microphone is off, tap OPEN SETTINGS and allow it.
  • How do I remove my voice from shared clips? On the share screen, tap the microphone next to SHARE. It says GAME SOUNDS ONLY when your voice is off.
  • How does Duo mode work? Both players need the app. One taps DUO and hosts a room, and the other joins with the room code. You need an internet connection.
  • I'm stuck on a level. After 5 deaths a lightbulb gives you a hint. After 10, SHOW ME plays the level for you. And SKIP is always there.
  • How do I turn off reminders? iOS Settings > TRUST ME > Notifications.
  • How do I reset my progress? Settings > RESET PROGRESS (tap twice). Your purchase stays; restore it any time.
